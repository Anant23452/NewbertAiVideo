GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Languages, video length and pilot access.

Can I create in Hindi and Hinglish? expand_more

Yes. Newbert natively generates scripts, voiceovers, and captions in conversational Hindi and Hinglish.

How long is a video? expand_more

Videos are typically 20 to 60 seconds, shaped directly by the narration within pilot limits.

Is the pilot unlimited? expand_more

No. Pilot access is rolling out in stages. Usage allowances and generation queues apply.

Can I publish directly to Instagram? expand_more

Direct social publishing is planned for a future release and is not available in the current prototype. You can download finished MP4 videos to share anywhere.
