# Newbert AI Video — homepage design brief

Date: 29 October 2026. Status: first Stitch concept for review.

Scope: public homepage only. Dashboard, creation screens, sign-in and settings come after homepage review. This does not implement or deploy application UI.

Stitch project: `projects/2520368196713015322`, Newbert AI Video — Homepage Design.

Review concept: `projects/2520368196713015322/screens/32f5415804cf45e2b261b203f7750e0e`, “Newbert AI Video - Homepage Refined”. [Open project in Stitch](https://stitch.withgoogle.com/projects/2520368196713015322).

Exports: [full-page screenshot](stitch-homepage-v1.png), [hero preview](stitch-homepage-v1-hero.png), and [Stitch HTML design export](stitch-homepage-v1.html). The HTML is an unintegrated design artifact, uses external Tailwind/fonts, and contains mock controls/illustrative player artwork. It is not wired to authentication, generation, or the local prototype videos.

First review corrections: removed invented psychology percentages, 4K/60fps and tempo claims, fictitious example counts, unsupported pronunciation/licensing guarantees and developer-facing counters. Corrected prototype durations to 39s and 21s, simplified benefit copy and kept pilot/Instagram limitations visible. Desktop concept only; homepage mobile treatment and other product screens await review.

Reference: the existing psychology spotlight-effect prototype in `artifacts/psychology-spotlight/video.mp4`, plus the binary-number prototype. The Stitch generation receives a written visual description; the local video has not been uploaded to Stitch.

Direction: black/near-black canvas, white geometric type, electric-violet rings and lighting, small ice-mint highlights, restrained lightning accent, explanatory diagrams and clear Hindi subtitles. No avatars or student homework dashboard imagery.

Page sequence: navigation; headline/topic composer with Hindi/Hinglish choice; vertical spotlight-effect preview; five-stage creation flow; two prototype examples; narration/graphics/captions/private-workspace benefits; honest FAQ; final action and footer.

Headline: “Turn your ideas into videos that explain.”

Main action: “Create a video.” Topic is preserved when continuing through sign-in. Prototype video is user-played, never autoplayed with audio.

Honesty requirements: label examples as prototypes; disclose pilot allowances/queues; Instagram publishing is planned, not released; no invented prices, unlimited generation, testimonials or usage numbers. No additional pages are requested.

Review: hero hierarchy, actual product relevance, topic entry clarity, diagram readability, Hindi rendering, contrast, alignment, mobile stacking, and whether the black/violet direction matches the videos. After desktop review, refine and produce the homepage's mobile treatment before moving to other product pages.
